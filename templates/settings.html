OTP retrieval strategy

  • Progressive checking: only unread messages are checked in the first 3 polls, then all messages are checked
  • Timestamp filtering: Automatically skip old emails before OTP is sent
  • OTP deduplication: Avoid reusing the same OTP
  • Multi-strategy extraction: subject priority -> semantic matching -> fallback matching
  • Sender validation: Verify that the email is from the official OpenAI domain
